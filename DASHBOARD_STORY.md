
# Dashboard Stakeholder Stories

## CITY OPS LEAD

### Headline
Banglore has highest SLA breach rate of 15.9% among the cities

### Evidence
Banglore has an SLA breach rate of 15.9% compared with 14.3% for the next highest city  Hyderabad
a diffrence of 1.6% points

### Implication
city ops lead should focus on identifying and adressing the operational factors
contributing to sla breach rate in banglore

## CATEGORY LEAD

### Headline
Deep home cleaning is the strongest category while salon for women has 
lower booking volume

### Evidence
Deep home cleaning generated INR 508,964 in revenue from 176 bookings.
In comparison Salon for women generated INR 151,689 in revenue from 65 bookings.


### Implication
The category lead can continue to monitor Deep home cleaning as a key revenue and booking contributor,
while reviewing salon for women to identify oppurtunities to increase demand.
